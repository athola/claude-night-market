# Capturing Expert Knowledge Before It's Lost (Dozuki)

Dozuki's playbook for preserving expert knowledge ahead of manufacturing retirements: capture-in-action with media, centralized troubleshooting repos, co-mentorship, accessible digital work instructions, and structured tribal-knowledge surfacing. Practitioner methods for expert-knowledge capture programs.

## Key points

- Record processes in action (photos/video) instead of documenting from memory
- Centralized troubleshooting repositories for recurring failures
- Co-mentorship pairing retiring experts with digitally-native juniors
- Accessible digital work instructions replacing paper/legacy systems
- Structured Q&A locations to surface implicit/tribal knowledge
