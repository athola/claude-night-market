# Top 3 Challenges Facing the Skilled Trades (Canada)

Industry overview of skilled-trades challenges in Canada: large worker deficits, a retirement wave eroding institutional knowledge, recruitment stigma, and technology-driven upskilling pressure. Context for why tacit knowledge-transfer tooling matters in trades workforces.

## Key points

- ~10,000-worker deficit across 56 Red Seal trades over five years
- 700,000+ skilled-trades workers expected to retire by 2028
- Persistent stigma hampers recruitment of younger workers
- 25% of ~4M tradespeople need upskilling within five years from digital disruption
- Effective transfer needs pipeline, intergenerational retention, and adaptive training
