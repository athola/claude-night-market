# Demystifying Tacit Knowledge in Graphic Design (Son et al., 2024)

Son, Choi, Kim & Kim characterize tacit knowledge in graphic design: a characteristics framework, 123 coded instances from 10 designers, a review of underaddressed tool support, and guidelines for embedding hard-to-articulate expertise into computational tools. Enables structured design of tacit-knowledge capture features.

## Key points

- Framework of tacit-knowledge characteristics from literature + qualitative analysis
- 123 tacit-knowledge instances coded from interviews with 10 pro designers
- Identifies elements, actions, and purposes of tacit knowledge
- Reviews prior tool support to find underaddressed tacit-knowledge instances
- Proposes guidelines for capturing/communicating tacit knowledge in tools
