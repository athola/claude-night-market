# Cloneable: agentic AI cloning expert-worker knowledge

Crunchbase profile of Cloneable: agentic AI that observes expert workers and clones their workflows into small specialized models for utilities/infrastructure facing retirement-driven shortages, with large claimed throughput gains and a $4.6M raise. Case study in observation-based tacit-expertise capture (claims unverified).

## Key points

- Observes experts performing tasks, replicates know-how into autonomous agents
- Targets utilities/infrastructure facing ~2.4:1 retirement-to-entry ratios
- Shadows experts to capture audio/docs, trains small specialized models
- Claims 8h engineer tasks done in <2min; 2-3M poles/agent/yr vs ~5k human
- $4.6M raise (Congruent Ventures); AEP, SoCal Edison customers
